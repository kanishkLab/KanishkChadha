Logo & Monogram

Construction

The monogram is an Inter 800 lettermark — K in ink, C in Signal Orange — set tight at −0.08em so the letters nest. The wordmark uses Inter 700 at −0.04em.

Clear space & sizing

Keep clear space equal to the cap-height of the mark on all sides. Minimum 20px digital / 7mm print. On navy, K reverses to white; C stays orange.

Do & Don't
Do — keep K in ink or white and C in orange. Place on navy, white, or off-white.
Don't — swap the letter colours, add a box outline, stretch, or set on busy photography.
