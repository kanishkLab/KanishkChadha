Voice & tone

The voice

Direct, practical, and confident. Short declaratives. Lead with the outcome, not the tool. Teach, don't sell — the reader should leave able to do something.

Say — "Outcomes, not project names." · "The eval that proves it's right."
Avoid — hype adjectives, buzzword stacks, vague "synergy" claims, and exclamation marks.
